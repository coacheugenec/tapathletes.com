TAP Athletes — logo system

The mark is a baseball seam — the stitches a pitcher grips — arcing over the wordmark. No diamond, no home plate. Bold condensed type (Barlow Condensed), all converted to vector outlines. Built on the live-site palette: ink on cream with one bold accent. Direction informed by the open-design / Nike "radical simplicity — monochrome + single accent" approach.
